# 3D Printing Resources

## Knowledge

- [NIOSH: Approaches to safe 3D printing](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/docs/2024-103/default.html)
  Primary safety source for small workspaces, makerspaces, schools, and libraries. Use for: ventilation, exposure controls, printer placement, and safe operating habits.
- [EPA: 3D Printing Research](https://www.epa.gov/chemical-research/3d-printing-research-epa)
  Government research summary on VOCs and ultrafine particles from filament printing. Use for: why emissions matter and why material choice is part of safety.
- [American Lung Association: 3D Printers](https://www.lung.org/clean-air/indoor-air/indoor-air-pollutants/3d-printers)
  Current indoor-air guidance for home users. Use for: practical controls such as source control, ventilation, filtration, and avoiding occupied spaces.
- [CPSC: Additive Manufacturing / 3D Printing](https://www.cpsc.gov/Regulations-Laws--Standards/Voluntary-Standards/Additive-Manufacturing-3D-Printing)
  Standards-oriented safety reference, including UL 2904 emissions testing. Use for: understanding why certifications and emissions claims matter.
- [Prusa Knowledge Base: Printer Handbooks](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/printer-handbooks_125045)
  Manufacturer-maintained manuals covering setup, calibration, slicing, printing, and troubleshooting. Use for: the full beginner operating path.
- [Prusa Knowledge Base: PLA](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/pla_2062)
  Clear material reference explaining why PLA is beginner-friendly and where it is weak. Use for: first filament choice.
- [Prusa Academy](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/prusa-academy_326256)
  Structured beginner courses and official training materials. Use for: a guided path after buying or borrowing a printer.
- [Bambu Lab A1 mini official specs](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/a1-mini)
  Current manufacturer specs for a low-friction beginner printer option. Use for: evaluating small build volume, automatic calibration, supported materials, and current pricing.
- [Bambu Lab A1 official specs](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/A1/)
  Current manufacturer specs for a larger beginner-friendly printer option. Use for: evaluating build volume, automation, remote monitoring, and material limits.
- [Creality Ender-3 V3 comparison](https://www.creality.com/compare/compare-ender-3-v3-series)
  Current manufacturer comparison for budget-oriented Ender options. Use for: evaluating what lower price trades away.
- [Original Prusa MINI+ official product page](https://www.prusa3d.com/product/original-prusa-mini/)
  Current manufacturer page for a compact, repairable printer with strong documentation. Use for: support, repairability, and ecosystem evaluation.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [MakerLabs Vancouver membership](https://www.makerlabs.com/membership)
  Local makerspace with 3D printing access and tool orientation. Use for: trying printing before buying and getting human feedback.
- [North Vancouver District Public Library StoryLab 3D printing](https://www.nvdpl.ca/storylab-3d-printing)
  Local library service that prints user-submitted designs. Use for: testing whether the hobby fits before buying hardware.
- [Richmond Public Library 3D Printing](https://www.rhpl.ca/library-services/creative-studio/3d-printing)
  Local-ish public 3D printing program with required 3D Printing 101 workshop. Use for: hands-on orientation and beginner training.
- [Printables](https://www.printables.com/)
  Large model community with ratings, photos, remixes, and many practical prints. Use for: choosing starter projects with evidence that other users succeeded.
- [Tinkercad](https://www.tinkercad.com/)
  Beginner-friendly browser CAD tool. Use for: first custom parts before moving to Fusion, Onshape, or Blender.

## Gaps

- Need the user's target use cases before recommending a specific printer.
- Need the user's available printer location, noise tolerance, and ventilation options.
- Need budget range, including supplies and replacement parts, not just printer price.
