# Mission: Learn 3D Printing

## Why
I want to decide whether to obtain a 3D printer, choose the right kind of starter setup, and get enough practical fluency to make useful first prints without wasting money or creating unsafe habits.

## Success looks like
- I can decide whether to buy, borrow, or use a local makerspace printer first.
- I can compare beginner FDM printers using safety, space, support, materials, and total cost.
- I can complete a first-month project ladder: test print, useful downloaded model, modified model, and first simple custom part.

## Constraints
- Start with beginner-friendly FDM printing, especially PLA and PETG.
- Treat ventilation, printer placement, and fire/electrical safety as first-class buying criteria.
- Prefer practical projects over decorative-only novelty prints.

## Out of scope
- Resin printing, unless the mission changes later.
- Engineering-grade materials such as nylon, polycarbonate, ASA, ABS, and fiber-filled filaments as first-month goals.
