Agentic Workflows

Eight short lessons for experienced engineers who want to design, build, and evaluate agents. Each lesson takes about ten to twelve minutes and covers one idea.

Lessons
  1. 01 The Agent LoopTrace one run of the model, tool, and result cycle, and find where it stops. ~12 min
  2. 02 Workflows Versus AgentsDecide who controls the next step, and choose the simplest design that works. ~10 min
  3. 03 Designing ToolsWrite tool definitions that an agent uses correctly on the first attempt. ~12 min
  4. 04 Context and StateManage the finite context window with four techniques for long tasks. ~12 min
  5. 05 Guardrails and ApprovalAdd budgets, permissions, and a human approval gate to write actions. ~12 min
  6. 06 Evaluating AgentsWrite an eval with a grader, run it repeatedly, and read the result as a rate. ~12 min
  7. 07 Workflow PatternsChoose among chaining, routing, parallelization, orchestration, and evaluation loops. ~12 min
  8. 08 Multi-Agent SystemsDecide when sub-agents are worth their cost, and defend the choice in writing. ~10 min
