What a warehouse promises

Lesson 3 of 8. Time: 10 minutes. Tangible win: explain ACID and group the eight warehouse capabilities.

The paper lists what a warehouse can do, then later shows a lakehouse doing the same. Group the capabilities so you can recall them. Source: Sections 2.2 and 2.3.

Eight capabilities in three groups

GroupCapabilities
Speed and scaleLow query latency, high concurrency, ad hoc queries, workload management, separation of storage and compute
SafetyGovernance and security, ACID-compliant transactions
FlexibilitySchema and physical layout evolution

ACID in plain words

LetterGuarantee
AtomicityA statement runs completely or not at all.
ConsistencyChanges in a transaction behave as expected.
IsolationConcurrent transactions do not interfere.
DurabilityCommitted changes persist.

Three transaction features matter later: multi-statement and multi-table transactions, rollback, and a locking model.

Practices that are not tied to technology

Data modeling, ETL or ELT (move data in, transform before or after loading), and data quality. Data quality includes master data management, referential integrity, and slowly changing dimensions, which track changing attributes such as a customer's address while keeping history.

Why multi-table matters

The paper's example: a data load often spans several tables. If one table's changes are visible downstream before the rest, results become inconsistent. Multi-statement transactions prevent that.

Retrieval check

A load fails halfway and no rows from it appear. Which ACID property is at work?

Correct. Atomicity means the whole statement runs or none of it does.

Not quite. Isolation concerns concurrent transactions and durability concerns persistence. All-or-nothing behavior is atomicity.

Practice

  1. Recite the three capability groups and one example from each.
  2. Explain why a dashboard reader needs isolation while a load job writes.
  3. Give your own example of a slowly changing dimension.
Primary source The Data Lakehouse: Data Warehousing and More, Mazumdar, Hughes, Onofré (arXiv 2310.08697). Read Sections 2.2 and 2.3 (Capabilities and practices). About 5 minutes.
