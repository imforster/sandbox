Primary source

Book

Kahneman, D. Thinking, Fast and Slow (2011). Read Part I for the System 1 and System 2 framework, then the heuristics and biases chapters in Part II. Check all section references against your own copy.

Peer-reviewed research

  • Goddard et al., systematic review of automation bias in decision support. Reviews 74 studies on frequency, mediating factors, and mitigations. Lessons 0001, 0003, 0010.
  • Sharma et al., Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models (arXiv 2310.13548, ICLR 2024). Lesson 0007.

Cognitive surrender research

  • Shaw and Nave, Thinking, Fast, Slow, and Artificial (SSRN 6097646, 2025). Working paper, Wharton. Three experiments, 1,372 participants. Lessons 0001, 0010.
  • Interactive explainer of Shaw and Nave by Avinash Mohan Chetty. Secondary; check numbers against the paper.

Preprints (verify before citing)

  • Huang et al., anchoring effect across LLMs (arXiv 2505.15392, later accepted to ICLR 2026). Lesson 0002.
  • Valencia-Clavijo, mechanistic study of anchoring in small open models (arXiv 2511.05766). Lesson 0002.
  • When Wording Steers the Evaluation (arXiv 2601.13537, 2026). Framing bias in 14 LLM judges. Lesson 0005.
  • More or Less Wrong, MathComp benchmark (arXiv 2506.03923). Framing and directional bias across GPT, Claude, and Qwen. Lesson 0005.
  • Provenance density and the transparency penalty (arXiv 2609.03460, 2026). High-fluency hallucinations. Lesson 0006.
  • Metacognitive decoupling and AI explanations (arXiv 2603.29681, 2026). Explanation length and confidence. Lesson 0008.
  • To Rely or Not to Rely (arXiv 2412.15584, 2024). Interventions for LLM reliance. Lesson 0008.

Secondary sources (lower trust)

  • Ulrich Boser, 5 Tips To Keep Your Brain From Surrendering To AI (Forbes, 2026). Practitioner tips. Lesson 0010.
  • LessWrong wiki, Thinking, Fast and Slow. Orientation only.
  • Automation bias, Wikipedia. Check its citations.
  • Representativeness heuristic, Wikipedia. Check its citations.
  • A 2025 Science study on sycophantic AI responses (2,405 participants). Reported second-hand; verify the original authors and findings before citing. Lesson 0007.
