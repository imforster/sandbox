# Resources

Ordered by trust. The browsable version is at `reference/resources.html`.

## Primary source

- **Thinking, Fast and Slow**, Daniel Kahneman (2011). Part I for the System 1 and System 2 framework, then the heuristics and biases chapters in Part II. Check section references against your copy.

## Peer-reviewed research

- Goddard et al., systematic review of automation bias (PMC7651899). Lessons 0001, 0003, 0010.
- Sharma et al., sycophancy in language models (arXiv 2310.13548, ICLR 2024). Lesson 0007.
