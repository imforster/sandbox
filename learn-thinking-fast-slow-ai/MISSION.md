# Mission

**Topic:** Applying Kahneman's dual-process ideas (System 1 and System 2, heuristics and biases) to how people interact with AI.

**Why:** Improve my own AI use. I want to notice when my fast, intuitive thinking is accepting or rejecting AI output without checking it, and set up my prompts and evaluation habits to counter that.

**Starting point:** New to the material. I know the rough System 1 / System 2 split but not the details.

**Success looks like:** I can name the bias at play when I catch myself over-trusting or dismissing an AI answer, and I have a repeatable check I run before acting on AI output.
